# Checking the login-page service indicators

## Steps

1. On the opening login screen, select Services to open Service status.
2. Review the separate Internet, Auth, Database, API and Storage rows and their explanations.
3. Check the last-check time and select Refresh if the result is old or your connection changed.
4. Close the panel with its close control, Escape or an outside click. You can still attempt sign-in/password reset while indicators are checking.

## Colors

Green means no reported issue, yellow means checking/limited/unknown/degraded, and red means the device appears offline or a provider reports an outage. The overall dot shows the most severe state. Old results become unverified rather than staying green indefinitely. Checks repeat while signed out and on relevant connection/focus changes.

## What the indicators cannot prove

Internet is checked from this device; other rows use public global provider reports. Green does not verify your password, staff profile, business permission, a particular CERMS deployment, receipt printer or card terminal. An endpoint blocked on your network can also affect a probe. A yellow/unknown report is not proof of healthy service or a confirmed outage. Use the actual sign-in/action error and contact support with its version/time if it persists; do not change credentials or records solely because a status dot changed.
