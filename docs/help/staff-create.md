# Creating a staff login account

## Before you start

Only the designated owner creates staff logins or grants permissions. This is Users & permissions, not customer membership creation. Verify the selected business and use a new login email; do not try to reassign or reset an existing shared account through creation.

## Steps

1. Open Users & permissions and select Create staff account.
2. Enter the staff member's name, intended login email and an initial password of 12–128 characters.
3. Review each business permission rather than assuming a cashier role grants everything. Use point of sale, refunds, product management, staff administration and register rights are separate.
4. Review the new account details and confirm creation once.
5. Wait for confirmation. Provide access details through your business's private procedure, not an AI chat or public support issue.
6. Have the staff member sign in and check their business/available controls. They can use Account settings for their own verified email/password changes.

## If blocked

An email already used by a shared login is not automatically linked, reset or reassigned. Ask the owner/support to review the existing account rather than creating an unrelated customer. If the result is uncertain, use the same creation retry offered by the app. Do not submit repeated new accounts. A staff profile email, login email and customer email are different concepts and should not be treated interchangeably.
