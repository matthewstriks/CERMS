# Recording cash, card and split payments

## Before you start

Finish the cart, note reviews, discounts and recipient details. Use your own open register. Process any real card/gift-card transaction on the external terminal/service; CERMS only records it and does not maintain gift-card balances.

## Steps

1. Select Take payment and review the final amount due.
2. Choose the actual payment method and enter its amount. Add another payment row for split tender when required.
3. For cash, enter the amount handed over. CERMS calculates applied cash and change; verify both before returning change.
4. For card or another external method, record only payment actually received and any supported reference. Do not enter full card numbers or security codes in notes/references.
5. Confirm the balance is fully settled and select Complete sale once.
6. Wait for the saved receipt, then choose Print, Email, Email + print or None.

## If the response is uncertain

Do not charge the customer again, repeat cash collection or start a replacement order. Keep the original order and use Retry same order/its offered retry action. A declined external card payment is not fixed by recording it as paid. A printer/email failure after the receipt is saved does not undo the sale; retry only that delivery action. Discounts can make an order zero; still review and complete its authorized fulfillment once.
