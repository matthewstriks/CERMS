# Staff accounts and permissions

## View or change rights

1. Open Users & permissions. Ordinary staff can inspect their effective rights; the owner can select a staff account to view/change business permissions.
2. The owner adjusts the required flags and selects Save permissions. Other staff administrators cannot grant themselves or others extra rights.
3. Refresh/sign out and in if controls still show old permissions. Server actions recheck saved rights, so an old enabled button does not guarantee authorization.

## Permission meanings

- Use point of sale: sales, membership/rental transactions, active-rental management and own-register cash drops. Does not grant refunds.
- Process returns and refunds: original-receipt refunds and their deliberate stock/service options.
- Manage discount codes: promotion configuration; applying a known code uses POS rights.
- Waive products: complimentary eligible product lines with selling access.
- Edit member records / Delete member records: separate customer management rights; edit also allows member-file upload and DNA changes.
- Remove member notes: separate from adding notes and clearing DNA.
- Create and edit products and categories / Delete products: independent catalog rights.
- Open a register / Close own register / Close other staff’s registers: separate drawer actions.
- Edit staff profiles / Remove staff access: CERMS 5 profile administration, not shared-login credential editing.

## If denied

Check the selected business and ask the owner for the specific needed permission. New optional rights default off for non-owner staff. Changing a name/email/rank does not create owner access. General business reads and private-message participation are not an unrestricted admin override; the owner cannot read a private chat without participating.
