# Editing staff profiles and removing CERMS 5 access

## Before you start

The owner may delegate Edit staff profiles and Remove staff access separately. Both apply only in your saved business. Creating logins and granting rights remain owner-only. This administration changes CERMS 5 profiles/access, not another person's shared login password/email or legacy access.

## Edit a profile

1. Open Users & permissions and locate the staff member.
2. Choose Edit profile and check the staff identity.
3. Change the CERMS 5 name or profile email, then save and wait for confirmation.
4. Explain that profile email is not the login email. The person changes their own verified login email in Account settings.

## Remove access

1. Ensure the person's open register is closed through the authorized closeout process.
2. Choose Remove access on the correct staff row.
3. Review the warning and confirm only when intended. Wait for confirmation.
4. The person loses CERMS 5 profile/business access; their shared login and legacy CERMS account remain intact. A loaded screen may retain old information until refreshed.

## Protections and recovery

You cannot remove yourself or the protected owner; delegated admins cannot edit the owner. Removal does not erase receipts, notes or historical attribution. Reload stale profile versions before reapplying edits. Do not delete a customer membership to remove an employee login, and do not expect a profile-email edit to solve a forgotten shared password.
