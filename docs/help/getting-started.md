# Getting started and finding the right screen

## Before you start

Use a staff login supplied by the owner. Customer memberships do not provide staff access. Most business operations need internet. Available controls depend on your permissions and selected business.

## Steps

1. Sign in and check the business shown in the sidebar. Stop if it is not the business you intend to use.
2. Use Overview for current rental activity and alerts; Admissions & rentals for active visits; Members for customer records; Point of sale for orders and receipts; Registers for drawers; Products for the catalog; Messages for staff conversations; Reports & analytics for reporting; Settings for your account, sounds and updates.
3. Open Users & permissions to inspect your rights or, when authorized, manage staff. A disabled button does not grant permission when clicked.
4. Before ending a shift, resolve uncertain payments/refunds, finish rental actions and close your register as required by your business.
5. Use Sign out when leaving the workstation. Do not share a staff password or leave a signed-in drawer for another cashier.

## If something is missing

Check Settings for your version. Features described in newer documentation need the corresponding installed release. Ask the owner about permissions or unavailable business features; use CERMS Assistant for guided help, or the separate Support / Report a bug action for an application error. Sample/demo screens are not a substitute for live saved records.
