# Reviewing member details, history and activity logs

## Steps

1. Find the customer in Members and open View.
2. Check identity, plan, expiration, DNA/Read notes flags and contact details.
3. Review notes, recorded visits/orders and attachments in their sections. Use an attachment's own View/Download action.
4. Where available, open the member activity log beneath notes to inspect recorded creation/view events. Entries show recorded staff attribution and time when available.
5. For a financial question, open the saved receipt in POS History/Sales & payments or Reports; a profile's current plan is not a financial ledger.

## Reading older information

Some historical entries lack a timestamp, attribution or a linked modern receipt. CERMS does not invent those values. History sections may indicate that more records exist or that a source could not load; do not treat a capped/unavailable list as the person's complete history.

## Limits

The activity log is not a full audit of every field change. View logging is currently limited to its supported Dev/owner flow; merely lacking a view event does not prove no one opened the record. Deleting a member does not delete separate receipt/visit/file history. A refunded or expired membership is also different from a deleted member. Ask the owner to review discrepancies rather than editing history to make totals agree.
