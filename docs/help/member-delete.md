# Deleting a member record

## Before you start

You need Delete member records permission. Deletion is permanent and is not a refund, rental checkout or membership cancellation. A checked-in member cannot be deleted.

## Steps

1. Locate the correct member and review their name, membership number and ID details.
2. Resolve any current visits through the normal rental checkout process before deletion. Do not use deletion to end a visit.
3. Open Delete member and read the confirmation carefully.
4. Confirm Delete permanently only when authorized by your business. Cancel if you are uncertain.
5. Wait for the result and refresh the member list.

## What remains

Deleting the member removes that customer record, including embedded fields. It does not erase separate receipts, rental/order history, view logs or stored files, and does not issue money back. Retained data may no longer be reachable through the deleted profile's screen. Managed membership numbers remain reserved and are not recycled.

## If refused

Check permissions and admission state, then refresh for changes made by another staff member. Never attempt to bypass a checked-in restriction. Use Returns/refunds for money back, or the membership-return cancellation option when appropriate, rather than deleting the customer to reverse a sale.
