# Counting and closing your own register

## Before you start

You need Close own register permission and your own open drawer. Resolve uncertain orders/refunds/cash drops first. Closing blocks new transactions on that register.

## Steps

1. Open Registers and choose Close my register/the own-register close action.
2. Count actual ending bills and coins, then enter every denomination quantity.
3. Review the calculated counted cash, expected cash, cash drops/refunds and over/short. Add a closing note where appropriate.
4. Confirm only after checking counts. Wait for saved closure.
5. Review the offered Register closeout report and print deliberately if required. You can reprint a closed register later.

## Understanding cash

Expected cash reflects opening cash and this shift's recorded cash sales/change, payouts, refunds and drops. A cash drop reduces expected cash; it is not automatically a shortage. Counted minus expected determines over/short. Card totals are recorded separately and do not add physical cash.

## If wrong or failed

Correct unsaved denomination entries before confirming. Once saved, opening/closing counts and the closed record are immutable. A print failure does not reopen the register; retry the report printing only. If save is uncertain, refresh/check the saved state before another close attempt. A manager's permission to close another person's drawer is independent of own-close permission.
