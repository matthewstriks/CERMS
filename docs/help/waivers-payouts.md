# Waiving a product or recording a payout

## Product waiver steps

1. With Use point of sale and Waive products permission, build the order normally.
2. Choose Waive on the eligible product line. Review the zero charge and waived indicator before payment. Use Unwaive to restore the normal charge before checkout.
3. Complete the sale even when all charges are zero so the receipt and any membership/rental fulfillment are recorded once.

## Payout steps

1. Add the configured payout product and enter a price when requested.
2. Review the outgoing amount and use Record payout for a negative order.
3. Record the external payout method/amount through the payment dialog and complete it once. Cash payouts must fit recorded drawer funds.

## Limits

A product waiver is a complimentary price decision, not a signed customer waiver document. Payout products cannot be waived. Payout orders cannot use discount codes and cannot be returned through the original-sale refund flow. A payout is not a substitute for Return / refund on a customer's original receipt. CERMS records money movement; it does not send a bank payment. Reports retain waived units/value and outgoing payment effects. If saving is uncertain, retry the original request rather than paying out twice.
