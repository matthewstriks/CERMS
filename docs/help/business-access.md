# Checking business access and changing system

## Before you start

Business records and staff permissions belong to the business shown in the workspace. Only the designated owner has Change system; changing a name, email or rank does not grant it.

## Owner steps

1. Finish or preserve current work and resolve pending transactions before changing business.
2. Select Change system under the current system in the sidebar.
3. Choose an existing system, confirm its name/identifier, then select Switch system. Opening or canceling the picker does not switch access.
4. Wait for the workspace to reload and verify the displayed business before doing any work. Searches, pages and dialogs from the previous workspace are discarded.

## Other staff

If the business is wrong or unavailable, contact the owner. You cannot switch by editing your own display name/email or by creating a customer. Signing in never assigns a different business automatically.

## If the switch is uncertain

Allow CERMS to recheck saved access. If it cannot confirm the business, it may sign out rather than show uncertain records. Sign in again when connected and check the displayed business. Do not assume an unconfirmed switch succeeded or try operations against the old screen.
