# Reports and daily emails

## Desktop report steps

1. Open Reports & analytics and verify business and the displayed workstation time zone.
2. Choose Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, This month or a custom inclusive date range of 1–366 days. Apply the controls and refresh as offered.
3. Review the applied dates and refresh time before interpreting results. Changing draft date controls does not relabel old results until applied.
4. Use Overview for financial totals/comparisons, Sales & payments for methods/products/cashiers/receipts, Memberships for the current base/follow-up, Rental activity for recorded starts/current visits, and Register closeouts for closed shifts.
5. Export the relevant complete loaded source as CSV when needed.

## Financial interpretation

The comparison is the immediately preceding equal number of local calendar days. No previous baseline is not an infinite improvement. Refunds are separate counts/amounts on their recording date and reduce net sales/tax/payment totals. Discounts are already included in paid totals; never subtract savings twice. Cash received and change differ from applied cash. External card totals do not prove bank settlement.

## If unavailable or different from email

An unavailable source is not zero. Retry/refresh or narrow an oversized financial range; independent sources may still load. Daily email is a separate Dev-only previous-day report at 7 AM America/Chicago, which can differ from the desktop's local time-zone range. Use its dedicated guide for schedule and workbook interpretation.
