# Creating, editing and deleting products

## Before you start

Create and edit products and categories permits catalog editing. Delete products is separate; deleting a product does not refund old orders or delete customer history.

## Steps

1. Open Products and create a product or open an existing product's editor.
2. Enter name, category, price, description/barcode and applicable retail, membership, rental or payout settings.
3. For memberships/rentals configure their duration and unit. Review taxable, active, favorite, ask-for-price and staff-restriction settings.
4. For tracked stock enter inventory and optional Inventory warning; leave inventory untracked when appropriate. Review any image selection.
5. Save and wait for confirmation. Check the product in POS or the relevant membership/rental selector.
6. To remove a product, use Delete product with separate permission and confirm the selected item. Existing receipts and member snapshots remain intact.

## Troubleshooting

Inactive, restricted or zero-stock products can be unavailable in POS. A membership/rental product must have a valid duration to fulfill that service. Favorites appear above collapsible categories. Refresh stale editor/catalog data rather than overwriting another manager's change. Deleting a product removes it from future choices, not historical financial records or stored image files. Category deletion is not available. Changing a price or duration does not rewrite an already saved sale or previously purchased time.

Core product is a saved catalog flag; a separate manage-core permission is not implemented. Current product editing/deletion uses the assigned catalog permissions. Do not assume the flag itself grants a special right or blocks every authorized edit.
