# Reviewing a register's payments and cash ledger

## Steps

1. Open the relevant register/ledger view and verify business, original cashier and shift reference.
2. Review opening cash and saved transaction/payment totals. Distinguish applied cash from cash received and change given.
3. Review cash payouts, cash drops and cash refunds when explaining expected drawer cash.
4. Compare expected cash with the physical ending count during closure. Use the closeout report for the saved ending result.
5. For an individual discrepancy, inspect the original saved sale/refund receipt and its reference instead of altering totals.

## Interpretation

Card and other external tender are records, not bank settlement confirmations. Refunds post to the issuing drawer/date; they do not rewrite an older original drawer. Discounts are already reflected in paid totals and must not be subtracted twice. Drops affect expected cash without creating a sale. Shift totals may span multiple calendar days and differ from a single-day sales report.

## Older/unavailable records

A register with no modern POS ledger may fall back to its saved opening cash for expected cash; CERMS does not invent legacy movements. An unavailable ledger is not a zero balance. Reconnect/retry the read and ask the owner to review inconsistent saved data. Closed counts/totals cannot be edited through this view, and manually changing member/product information does not repair a financial discrepancy.
