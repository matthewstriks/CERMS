# Confirming a customer's receipt email

## Before you start

Email/Email + print is offered when the order's primary linked member has an email on file. An unlinked customer label or another group recipient's email does not supply the primary recipient. This is deliberate receipt delivery, not an automatic customer mailing.

## Steps

1. After the order succeeds, choose Email or Email + print.
2. In the confirmation window, show/check the prefilled customer address.
3. Edit that address if needed for this receipt and confirm the send. The override does not edit the member's stored contact email.
4. Wait for email confirmation. For Email + print, finish the separate printing step.
5. Choose None or cancel the email confirmation to send nothing from that confirmation.

## If delivery fails

A printing failure after email success should retry printing only. Do not resend the email or repeat checkout. Email is limited to one confirmed delivery per order; the app protects identical retries. An uncertain email result can require support review because automatically sending again could duplicate delivery. Check the intended inbox/spam, but do not tell the customer it arrived merely because the app submitted it. Updating a member's email later does not modify a past delivery address or automatically resend old receipts.
