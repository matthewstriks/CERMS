# Reviewing sales, payment methods, products and cashiers

## Steps

1. In Reports & analytics apply the intended date range and open Sales & payments.
2. Review incoming/outgoing/net method totals, product performance and cashier performance. Products use saved receipt snapshots, not today's renamed/priced catalog.
3. Search/page the receipt details and open the original receipt for its lines, tax, payment methods, discounts or linked refund. Use the existing print action if a copy is needed.
4. Export products, cashiers, payment methods or receipt detail using the corresponding CSV choice.

## Reconciliation

Refund receipts reduce net money/product units without counting as new membership/rental purchases. They belong to the refund date and issuing register. A return uses the original paid discount/tax allocation. Payouts are outgoing records; cash handed over includes change, so it need not equal applied cash. A current product edit/deletion does not rewrite a saved receipt.

## POS History distinction

POS → Sales & payments/History filters apply to the receipts loaded there; load older receipts to extend coverage. Reports & analytics instead loads the applied report source through pagination, with explicit source errors/limits. Neither a partial History list nor the current visible table page is a complete-business total. Investigate a discrepancy using matching dates/time zone, register and original receipt references before changing business records.
