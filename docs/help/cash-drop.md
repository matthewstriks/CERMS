# Recording a cash drop during a shift

## Before you start

Use POS permission and your own open register. A cash drop records cash physically removed from the drawer; it is not a product sale, customer refund or expense integration.

## Steps

1. Count the cash being removed according to your business procedure.
2. Open the available Cash drop action for your open drawer in POS/register controls.
3. Enter a positive dollar amount and optional explanatory note.
4. Review the amount against the physical removal and recorded available drawer cash, then confirm once.
5. Wait for confirmation and review the ledger entry/updated expected cash.

## Result

The drop is recorded in register history and subtracted from expected closing cash, so a recorded safe drop is not counted as a shortage. It does not change sales revenue or move funds through a bank. Saved cash drops cannot be edited/deleted.

## If uncertain

The dialog retains the original amount/note/request for its retry, including after it is closed and reopened. Reconnect and retry the same pending drop; do not enter another drop for the same physical removal. A drop larger than recorded available cash is refused. Resolve an unconfirmed drop before closing the register. Report a mistaken saved amount to the owner rather than inventing compensating sales or editing shift history.
