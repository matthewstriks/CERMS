# Changing appearance and notification sounds

## Appearance

1. While signed in, use the moon/theme control at the top right to switch light/dark appearance.
2. The deliberate choice is saved to your CERMS 5 account. Accounts without a saved preference begin in light mode.
3. If saving fails, check the connection and retry; do not assume another account has the same preference.

## Sounds

1. Open Settings and find notification sound controls.
2. Set enabled/muted and volume. Select Test sound to preview the ordinary chime or Test error sound to preview the error tone.
3. Save the preference and wait for confirmation. Previews use the displayed draft volume and can play even while the saved preference is muted; testing does not save it.

## What to expect

Normal notifications/messages use the standard rising chime. Errors use a descending tone. Nearby bursts are combined and errors take priority, so every visible update does not necessarily produce a separate sound. Sounds need a signed-in session and may require interaction with the app before audio is allowed.

## If silent

Check the saved enabled setting, app volume, operating-system volume and output device. Zero volume/mute suppress ordinary playback. Do not change business or notification permissions to fix a speaker problem. This feature does not add external push notifications.
