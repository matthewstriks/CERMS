# Reporting on memberships and rental activity

## Membership steps

1. Apply the date range in Reports & analytics and open Memberships.
2. Review the current active, expired, DNA and unknown-expiration base. DNA is counted separately from the other statuses.
3. Use upcoming 7-day expirations and the 30-day expiring follow-up list to plan contact, following your business procedure.
4. Distinguish current records created in the dates from membership purchases/renewals recorded in completed orders. Export upcoming expirations when needed; this does not automatically email customers.

## Rental steps

1. Open Rental activity and review starts by day/hour/type plus distinct member arrivals.
2. Compare these dated starts with current active/inside/waiting/overdue counts. Multiple units for one person count separately.
3. Export daily rental starts if needed. Activity with no arrival date is excluded from dated totals and disclosed in a notice.

## Interpretation

Current member/live-rental counts are refreshed current snapshots regardless of the selected historical range. They do not reconstruct status or occupancy as of an old date. Renewing a rental adds time rather than another start; refunds are not new purchases. Deleted members are absent from the current base while immutable receipt purchase history remains. Expired membership does not by itself forbid the supported optional-renewal rental flow. Unknown/unavailable data must not be read as zero or proof of no activity.
