# Creating categories and organizing the POS catalog

## Before you start

Create and edit products and categories permission is required. Category organization is different from discount eligibility; changes should be reviewed with any category-based promotion.

## Steps

1. Open Products and its category controls.
2. Create a category or edit an existing category. Enter its name, description and color using the available fields, then save.
3. Open the relevant products and assign their category. Save each intended change.
4. In POS use the category filter to focus the catalog. Click a category heading to expand or collapse its product tiles.
5. Mark frequently used products Favorite in their product editor to show them above categories. Search/category restrictions and staff eligibility still apply.

## If a product is missing

Clear the search/category filter, expand its category, and check active/restricted/stock settings. A failed product image leaves a text tile; missing imagery does not necessarily mean the product is absent. Refresh the catalog after saved changes.

## Limits

Categories have no product-image upload field and cannot be deleted through the current app. Editing a category does not update already saved receipt snapshots. Do not create duplicate categories to work around a stale editor; reload and verify the existing list first.
