# Printing or emailing a receipt

## After an order

Choose Print, Email, Email + print or None. Email choices require the primary linked customer to have an email on file and open a separate address confirmation. None sends and prints nothing. Delivery does not repeat checkout.

## Print steps

1. Choose Print or Email + print after the saved order, or open a saved receipt from Sales & payments/History and choose its print action.
2. Review the receipt preview and select an operating-system-installed receipt printer.
3. Choose the correct paper width: 80 mm by default or 58 mm where applicable. Printer/width preferences are saved on this workstation.
4. Confirm printing through the offered print controls/system dialog. Canceling printing leaves the saved sale intact.

## If blank or not printing

For the Rongta RP326, first verify that the correct printer/driver is installed and can print from the operating system. Check paper, connection, selected device, paper size and the driver's cutter settings. CERMS does not install drivers or configure the cutter. Retry printing from the saved receipt; never repeat payment to fix a printer problem.

## Email distinction

Email delivery needs explicit confirmation of the displayed address and sends a receipt from saved order data. See Confirming a customer's receipt email for address edits and email-only retry rules. Printing success is separate from email success.
