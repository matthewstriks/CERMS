# Opening and closing a register

## Open your register

1. With Open a register permission, open Registers and choose the action to open your own drawer. POS also prompts in place when an order starts without an open register.
2. Count the actual opening bills/coins and enter quantities for each denomination, not dollar totals in quantity fields.
3. Review the calculated opening total and confirm. Each staff member may have one open register per business.
4. In a POS opening prompt, successful opening preserves the order draft and resumes its remaining dialogs. Canceling preserves the draft but leaves checkout unavailable until your register is open.

## Close your register

Use Close own register permission and the separate ending-count/closeout process. Count actual cash, review expected cash and over/short, and confirm closure. A saved closed register cannot be edited.

## If opening is blocked

Check business, permission and whether your own drawer is already open. Another person's open register cannot be used as your cashier register. The owner/manager must grant the proper permission; opening a drawer does not grant POS or refund permission. If confirmation is uncertain, reload the saved register state instead of opening a second drawer. Do not enter fake counts to bypass the prompt; only real denomination counts belong in a live shift.
