# Adding paid time to an active rental

## Before you start

You need POS permission, your own open register and an active same-business rental. Renewing a rental adds visit time; it does not renew the customer's membership.

## Steps

1. On Overview or Admissions & rentals, locate the visit and choose Renew through its available row/view action.
2. Review the linked member, rental and selected product/duration in the order. The renewal must stay attached to that specific visit.
3. Check the rest of the cart, recipient details and required member notes.
4. Record the external payment and complete the sale.
5. Verify the new expiration on the original active rental.

## How time is calculated

Purchased duration is added to the later of the rental's existing expiry and successful checkout time. Renew before expiry to retain remaining time; renew after expiry to start the added duration from checkout. Renewals do not create an extra rental start or admission. An already completed visit cannot be renewed.

## Recovery

Canceling or failed payment adds no time. An identical uncertain-sale retry cannot add time twice. If the target was checked out or changed by someone else, reopen/review it rather than substituting a different visit ID. Do not use in/out, notes or a room edit to change time. Returning a rental-renewal receipt with service cancellation ends the related rental rather than merely rolling its clock backward.
