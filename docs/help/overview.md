# Using Overview and Admissions & rentals

## Steps

1. Check the business shown in the workspace, then open Overview for current activity or Admissions & rentals for active visit rows.
2. Review the member, rental, Assigned Room/Number, time/expiry, inside status and waitlist information shown.
3. Use View for details. Authorized POS staff can Edit, Renew or Check out through the available actions.
4. Treat expired/overdue indicators as a prompt to review that visit. Expiry does not automatically record a completed checkout or a payment.
5. Use the appropriate action: Edit for assignment/notes/presence, Renew for purchased time, Check out for completion and recovered items.

## If information looks wrong

Check connection and the actual active visit rather than guessing from a stale row. Live subscriptions show active visits in the selected business and hide visits being made inactive. A person can have multiple rental units; count/review units separately. Outside does not mean checked out, waiting does not reserve a specific available room, and a free-text room assignment does not prove uniqueness. Membership expiry and rental expiry are different clocks. For historical activity and date totals, use Reports & analytics rather than interpreting current active counts as past occupancy.
