# Setting or clearing Do not admit (DNA)

## Before you start

You need Edit member records permission. DNA prevents the affected member's admission/eligible membership actions; reading or deleting a note does not override that restriction.

## Steps

1. Find the correct member, open View and review their notes.
2. In the notes section, change Do not admit (DNA).
3. When enabling it, enter the required DNA reason. The reason becomes a highlighted DNA · Do not admit note with staff attribution and time.
4. When clearing it, review the prior reasons and enter the optional reason for clearance.
5. Select Save DNA status and wait for confirmation. Verify the displayed state before continuing any order.

## What happens

The status and its attributed note are saved together. Clearing DNA records a separate clearance entry and retains earlier reasons. Removing a DNA reason from the note log does not clear DNA. The generic member editor cannot change this status directly.

## If the state changed

Reload the current member/notes and review the latest information before retrying. Do not acknowledge an old popup and assume it authorizes admission. If the person remains DNA, ask the authorized manager to resolve it; the AI cannot clear restrictions or approve admission.
