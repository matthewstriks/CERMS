# Understanding the daily report email and workbook

## Availability

The scheduled morning report currently covers Dev System only, at 7 AM America/Chicago for the previous day. Other businesses do not gain a report schedule by adding low-inventory recipients. This is separate from desktop CSV exports and does not require CERMS to remain open.

## Review steps

1. Check the email's business, reporting day/time zone and period before comparing it with a workstation report.
2. Read financial totals, payment methods, membership/rental purchase information, discounts, waivers, payouts and cash drops where included.
3. Open the attached Excel workbook for its detailed sheets, including orders/items and the dedicated Refunds worksheet.
4. Match a questioned amount with the original order/refund/register reference. Refunds are recorded on their refund date and issuing register, not by rewriting an earlier report day.
5. When comparing desktop Reports, use equivalent dates/time zone; the desktop follows the computer's displayed local zone.

## If missing or different

Check the expected configured recipient inbox/spam and ask the owner to review delivery. Inventory-email recipients do not configure daily reports. Do not change computer time or create test sales to provoke an email. An empty/refund-only day can still be reportable. QuickBooks integration, general scheduled-report configuration and arbitrary resend controls are not implemented in the desktop; ask the owner/support instead of following invented menu instructions.
