# Chatting with CERMS Assistant and finishing a conversation

## Steps

1. Open the CERMS Assistant help icon after setup, then describe the CERMS task/error without passwords, tokens or unnecessary customer details.
2. Read the proposed steps and expand Help articles to inspect the bundled documentation. AI cannot look up customers, change records, issue money or approve access.
3. Ask a follow-up if needed. For missing/uncertain instructions open the separate Report a bug / login Support button; using AI is never required to file one.
4. Use Finish chat or New chat when done. Closing the assistant window or quitting CERMS also ends the recorded conversation.

## Evaluation email

Chats may be monitored for quality assurance. Finished conversations are sent privately for review, including recorded questions/answers, article titles, app/device context and the staff name, login email, user ID and business captured when the chat starts. Signed-out chats are labeled as signed out; older conversations may have no recorded user. This automatic review report is separate from bug reports. Empty chats send none. Identity is a client-reported snapshot retained with offline delivery, not independent authentication by email. Signing out or changing business ends the old chat to avoid mixing users.

## Offline/close behavior

Pending transcripts are stored in a local retry journal until delivery is acknowledged. Normal quit attempts delivery; offline sends retry while CERMS runs or on its next launch. Force quit/power loss cannot send immediately from a stopped process; recovered drafts are handled next launch. Common secret patterns are redacted, but do not rely on redaction to make private text safe. AI answers can be wrong; an article citation is not proof that every generated sentence is correct.
